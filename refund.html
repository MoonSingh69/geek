Refund Policy

Last Updated: April 18, 2026

30-Day Return Policy

At Geek On Sale, your satisfaction is our priority. We offer a 30-day return policy from the date you receive your item. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, you may return it for a full refund or exchange.

Hassle-Free Returns: We want you to shop with confidence. Most items can be returned within 30 days.

Conditions for Returns

To be eligible for a return, please ensure:

  • Item is returned within 30 days of delivery
  • Item is in original condition (unused, undamaged)
  • All original packaging, accessories, and tags are included
  • Proof of purchase (order number or receipt) is provided
Restocking Fee: A 15% restocking fee may apply to opened electronics, computers, and gaming consoles.

Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

  • Gift cards and store credit
  • Final sale or clearance items (marked "Non-Returnable")
  • Downloadable software or digital products
  • Opened consumables (printer ink, toner, batteries)
  • Personal care items (earbuds, headphones for hygiene reasons if opened)
  • Items damaged due to misuse or improper handling

Refund Process

Once we receive and inspect your return, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. If approved:

  • Refunds are processed to the original payment method
  • Please allow 7-10 business days for the refund to appear on your statement
  • Original shipping charges are non-refundable (unless the item was defective or we made an error)
Processing Time: Returns are typically processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the item.

Return Shipping

Who pays for return shipping?

  • Customer's responsibility: If you changed your mind or ordered by mistake, you are responsible for return shipping costs.
  • Geek On Sale covers shipping: If the item is defective, damaged, or we sent the wrong item.

We recommend using a trackable shipping service and purchasing shipping insurance for items over $100. We are not responsible for returns lost in transit.

Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact us immediately within 48 hours of delivery. We will:

  • Provide a prepaid return shipping label
  • Send a replacement or issue a full refund
  • Cover all return shipping costs
Tip: Please take photos of any damage and email them to returns@Geekdeal.com for faster processing.

Exchanges

We offer exchanges for defective or damaged items. To exchange an item:

  • Contact our customer support within 30 days
  • Return the original item (we'll cover shipping)
  • Once received, we'll ship the replacement item at no extra cost

For size/color exchanges (non-defective), customers are responsible for return shipping, and a restocking fee may apply.

Cancellation Policy

Before shipment: You may cancel your order within 2 hours of placing it for a full refund. After 2 hours, please contact us immediately — we'll try to cancel, but if the order has already been processed for shipping, you'll need to initiate a return after receiving the item.

After shipment: Once shipped, you cannot cancel the order. Please wait to receive the package and then follow our return process.

Warranty Information

Most electronics come with a manufacturer's warranty. Please check the product page or user manual for specific warranty details. Geek On Sale does not provide extended warranties unless explicitly stated. For manufacturer warranty claims, please contact the brand directly or reach out to us for assistance.

Late or Missing Refunds

If you haven't received a refund after 10 business days of us approving it:

  • Check your bank account again
  • Contact your credit card company (it may take time to post)
  • Contact your bank (processing times vary)
  • If you've done all of the above and still haven't received your refund, please contact us at support@Geekdeal.com

Gift Returns

If you received an item as a gift and want to return it:

  • We can issue a store credit or gift card for the value of the item
  • Please provide the gift giver's order number if possible
  • Refunds cannot be issued to the original payment method for gift returns
